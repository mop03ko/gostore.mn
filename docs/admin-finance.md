# Admin financial report

The report filters by **order creation date in Ulaanbaatar**, then includes all recorded receipts, refunds and costs for those orders. It is an order cohort report, not a cash flow report filtered by payment date. Split shipment records have their own transport receipts and costs; they never repeat the parent goods payment or supplier purchase.

Cards separate initial invoice receipts, balance receipts, independently recorded cargo receipts, refunds, supplier cost, transport cost, other costs, FX difference and net profit. A balance invoice may include service or delivery charges. Its entire value is not assumed to be cargo: cargo is allocated from the final settlement only once it has been fully paid. Unsettled or partial balance allocation remains unknown.

Owners can enter finance data from Reports. Values live in private `meta` entries keyed `finance:<order id>`. Updates require authentication, CSRF, owner role and the expected ledger version. They are audited and never change customer invoices, payment status, warehouse balances or notifications. `extra_cargo_mnt` replaces the cumulative total of externally paid cargo **not already recorded by the system**. It requires a payment date and transaction reference/note. Do not enter QPay receipts again. Payments can be reconciled when supporting records are provided.

Supplier cost = (paid supplier CNY − confirmed supplier refund CNY) × actual CNY purchase rate. FX difference = supplier cost at the order's quoted rate − actual supplier cost. Positive FX means a gain, negative means a loss. Net profit = initial receipts + balance receipts + extra cargo − customer refunds − actual supplier cost − actual transport cost − other costs. FX is already included and is never added a second time. Other costs include allocated fees, tax and operating costs; the result is explicitly labeled profit from recorded costs, not an independently certified accounting profit.

Unknown values remain null, distinct from an explicit zero. A total cost/profit card remains unknown when any included order lacks the necessary data. The owner must confirm cost/refund reconciliation before a net profit is shown. Closed supplier orders require reconciliation; supplier refunds are entered separately from refunds paid back to customers. A financial CSV export includes all selected orders and marks missing values. Only the most recent 100 financial records are displayed in the table.

Validation: `tests/admin-reports.php` covers money calculations, refunds, unfavorable FX, negative profit, split isolation, missing costs, owner authorization, invalid inputs, stale edits and audit logging. `tests/finance-reports-ui.cjs` tests mobile/desktop rendering and editor submission; `tests/admin-extra-ui.cjs` tests the integrated report page and CSV export.
